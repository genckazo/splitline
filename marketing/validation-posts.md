## 1. Reddit — r/HomeNetworking (lead post)

**Title:** Two internet connections at home — why is there no simple app that puts games on one and downloads on the other?

**Body:**

I have two separate internet connections in the same house (two modems, two
different ISPs). All I want is stupidly simple:

- Connection A: only my game (and Discord)
- Connection B: downloads, uploads, torrents, updates — the stuff that murders ping

Right now my options seem to be: buy a dual-WAN router and spend a weekend
learning policy routing / mwan3 rules, pay a monthly subscription for a bonding
VPN that adds latency (the exact thing I'm trying to avoid), or use a bandwidth
limiter which doesn't actually move traffic to the other line.

Am I missing an obvious tool here? And if this genuinely doesn't exist as a
one-click thing — would anyone else actually use it, or is my setup just weird?

Asking because I'm considering building it, and I don't want to build something
only I need.

*(If people ask: I'm thinking one-time price, no subscription, no VPN servers,
runs locally.)*

## What counts as a signal (write results back here)

- Strong: comments saying "I need this", "shut up and take my money", people
  describing the same two-modem setup unprompted.
- Medium: upvotes/saves without comments.
- Weak/negative: "just use mwan3", "Speedify exists", "why would you have two
  connections" — those are the objections the product has to beat.
- Note the subreddit rules before posting; some ban idea-validation posts —
  the drafts above are written as genuine questions for that reason.
